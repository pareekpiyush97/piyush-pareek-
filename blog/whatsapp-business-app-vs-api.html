WhatsApp Business app vs WhatsApp Business API: which do you need?

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

Businesses in India hear about "WhatsApp automation" and get confused between the WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp Business API. They are different products for different needs. Here is a plain-language comparison.

In this article
  1. WhatsApp Business app
  2. WhatsApp Business API
  3. Quick comparison
  4. Which should you choose?
  5. Can Zrimat set it up?

WhatsApp Business app

A free app for one business owner or small team. It includes a business profile, product catalogue, quick replies, labels, and greeting and away messages. It works well for shops, clinics and service providers handling a moderate number of chats.

Good for: small businesses starting with WhatsApp.

WhatsApp Business API

Not an app you install, but a connection offered through approved providers. It lets software send and receive messages, so you can run automation, chatbots, CRM integration and multi-agent inboxes. Messaging charges and provider fees apply, and Meta's pricing rules change over time, so check current rates before you decide.

Good for: higher volume, automated flows, several staff on one number, or CRM integration.

Quick comparison

Business appBusiness API
SetupInstall and verifyProvider and approval needed
CostFree appProvider and message charges
AutomationBasic quick replies and away messagesFull workflows, chatbots, integrations
Team useLimitedMulti-agent inbox through software

Which should you choose?

Start with the Business app and the click-to-chat link on your website. When replies become repetitive, volume grows, or you need leads saved into a sheet or CRM automatically, move to the API or a lighter automation. Many small businesses never need the API.
