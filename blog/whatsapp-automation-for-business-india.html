WhatsApp automation for business in India: how it works

Zrimat · 12 November 2026 · 5 min read

Across India, most customers message a business on WhatsApp before they do anything else. The businesses that win are not the ones with the most staff — they are the ones that never miss a message and always follow up. That is what WhatsApp automation does.

The problem it solves

Enquiries come at all hours. A reply ten minutes late often means a lost customer, and manual follow-up almost never happens once the day gets busy. Automation closes both gaps.

How WhatsApp automation works

A good setup connects three things — your website, WhatsApp, and a record of every lead:

  • Capture — a form or button on your site sends the enquiry straight to your WhatsApp and a sheet.
  • Auto-reply — the customer gets an instant, correct response in Hindi or English without you typing.
  • Follow-up — if they go quiet, scheduled reminders go out on their own.

See the full setup on the WhatsApp automation service page.

The tools behind it

Most Indian small businesses run this with the WhatsApp Business API (or the app for smaller volumes) plus a workflow tool like n8n to connect everything. Add AI and the replies get smarter — that is covered under automation and AI workflows.

Who it is for

  • Businesses with more enquiries than the team can answer in time.
  • Clinics, salons and hotels that also need booking and reminders.
  • Anyone who loses leads to slow replies or forgotten follow-ups.

Frequently asked questions

How can I automate my WhatsApp Business account?

Start with the free WhatsApp Business app: greeting, away and quick-reply messages cover the basics. For automatic lead capture, follow-ups and replies connected to your website and Google Sheet, use the WhatsApp Business API with a workflow tool such as n8n.

Is WhatsApp automation for small business free?

Basic auto-replies in the WhatsApp Business app are free. Automation through the API depends on your provider plan plus message charges set by Meta, so the cost varies with usage.
