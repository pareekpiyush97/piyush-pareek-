Website design cost in Bikaner: what you really pay in 2026

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

If you run a shop, clinic, hotel or service business in Bikaner and are asking "how much does a website cost?", you will hear numbers from ₹3,000 to ₹3,00,000. Both can be honest answers, because they describe different things. This guide explains what moves the price, so you can compare quotes properly.

The short answer

For a standard business site, expect to pay a one-time amount in the range of ₹7,000 to ₹15,000 at Zrimat. Larger builds, such as an online store or an internal ERP/CRM system, are priced on the build because the catalogue, payment setup and number of modules change the work.

These are starting prices for a standard build. Pages, features and integrations change the final number, so ask for it in writing before work begins.

What actually changes the price

  • Number of pages. A five-page site takes less time than a twenty-page one.
  • Custom design vs template. A hand-coded layout built around your brand costs more than a template with your logo on it, and usually loads faster.
  • Features. Booking, forms that save to a sheet, WhatsApp routing and payment gateways each add work.
  • Content. If you need copywriting and photography, plan for it. Good photos of your own shop or rooms beat stock images.
  • Ongoing support. Updates after launch should be agreed up front, not discovered later.

Costs people forget to ask about

Ask every developer these four questions before you pay anything:

  1. Who owns the domain and the source code after launch?
  2. What is the yearly cost for the domain, and for hosting if any?
  3. What happens if I want a change in three months?
  4. Is the quote fixed, or can it grow?

The answer to the first one matters most. At Zrimat the source code stays on your own GitHub, so nothing is locked to us.

Cheap website vs good website

A very cheap site is not always a bad deal, but check three things: does it work properly on a phone, does it load quickly, and does it make it easy to contact you? Google recommends fast, mobile-friendly pages, and customers leave slow ones. A site that looks fine on a laptop but hides the phone number on mobile will cost you more in lost enquiries than you saved. See our 12-point website checklist to test any site, including your own.

How to get an accurate quote

  1. List the pages you need (home, services, gallery, contact).
  2. Decide whether customers should book, buy or just enquire.
  3. Collect your logo, photos and a few lines about your business.
  4. Ask for a fixed price and delivery date in writing.
