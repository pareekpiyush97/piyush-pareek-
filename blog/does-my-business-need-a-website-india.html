Does your small business need a website in India?

Zrimat · 12 November 2026 · 4 min read

Plenty of small businesses in India run entirely on Instagram or WhatsApp and do fine. So the honest question is not "is a website compulsory" — it is "what do you lose by not having one?"

What a website gives you that social does not

  • You own it. A social account can be restricted or banned overnight; your website is yours.
  • Google search. When someone searches your service in your city or across India, a website can show up — a profile usually cannot.
  • Trust. A real site with your work, prices and contact details converts far better than a feed.
  • Control of the journey. You decide how a visitor goes from interest to enquiry — not an algorithm.

When social-only is genuinely enough

If you sell occasionally to a small local circle and do not rely on new customers finding you, a well-run Instagram can carry you for a while. The moment you want steady enquiries from people who do not already follow you, that ceiling shows up fast. We went deeper on this in website vs Instagram for business.

When you clearly need a website

  • You want to be found on Google, not just on social.
  • You lose enquiries because there is no proper way to capture and follow them up.
  • You take bookings or orders and want them handled cleanly.
  • You want to look as serious as the competitors who already have a site.

How to start without overthinking it

You do not need a huge site. A clear homepage, your services, and WhatsApp lead capture is enough to start getting results. Build that first, then grow.
