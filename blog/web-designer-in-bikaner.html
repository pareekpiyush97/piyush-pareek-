Buyer's guide

Web designer in Bikaner: how to choose the right one

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

Bikaner has freelancers, small agencies and big-city companies all offering websites. The prices and promises look similar, but the results are not. Use this guide to compare web designers on things you can actually check, before you pay an advance.

Start with the portfolio, then open the sites

Do not judge by screenshots. Open each live site on your phone. Does it load quickly? Is the phone number or WhatsApp button easy to tap? Does the design look made for that business, or like a template with a new logo?

Ask which of the sites the designer built for businesses like yours. Zrimat's work includes a heritage hotel, a real estate business and a homeopathy clinic, all live for you to test.

Compare pricing the right way

Ask for a written quote that lists pages, features and delivery date. A low number that excludes forms, WhatsApp, Google setup or mobile design is not low at all. Published starting prices are a good sign. See what typical builds include in our website cost guide.

Seven questions to ask before you pay

  1. Who owns the domain, hosting account and source code?
  2. Is the site built for mobile first?
  3. How will customers contact me from the site (WhatsApp, form, call)?
  4. Will you set up Google Business Profile and basic SEO?
  5. What is included after launch, and for how long?
  6. Can I see the work live, not only screenshots?
  7. Who will I talk to during the project?

Freelancer, local agency or big-city company?

  • Freelancer: usually lower cost and direct contact. Check that they answer messages quickly and show real work.
  • Local agency: more people, often higher price. Ask who actually builds your site.
  • Big-city company: strong process, but you may pay for overheads you do not need.

For most shops, clinics and hotels in Bikaner, a hands-on builder who knows local customers is the best value, provided the answers above are clear.

Red flags

  • No live examples, or examples that are not theirs.
  • Refusing to give you the source code or domain access.
  • Promising "rank #1 on Google" in days. Nobody can guarantee that.
  • Asking for the full payment before showing any design.
