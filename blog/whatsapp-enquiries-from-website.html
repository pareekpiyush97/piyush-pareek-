How to get customer enquiries on WhatsApp from your website

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Most customers in India would rather send a WhatsApp message than fill a form or make a call. If your website only shows a phone number, you lose people who would have messaged. Here is how to make WhatsApp your main enquiry channel in four steps, with no paid tool.

Step 1: add a click-to-chat link

WhatsApp supports a simple link format. Replace the number with yours, written with the country code and no plus sign or spaces:

https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX

Put this behind a clear button such as "Chat on WhatsApp", in the header and again after each section. Tapping it opens the chat on mobile and WhatsApp Web on desktop.

Step 2: pre-fill the message

Add a text parameter so the customer does not stare at an empty box. Use %20 for spaces:

https://wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hi%20I%20want%20a%20quote%20for%20a%20website

Use a different message for each button, such as "Book a free call" or "Price for the Growth plan". You will instantly know what the customer wants when the message arrives.

Step 3: know where each lead came from

This is the step most small businesses skip. Add UTM tags to the links you share, for example on Instagram, Google Business Profile or a printed QR code:

https://yoursite.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=nov

A small script can save these values and append a short tag, like [src: instagram/bio/nov], to the pre-filled WhatsApp message. Now every lead tells you which channel worked. Zrimat sites include this by default.

Step 4: reply fast and follow up

A lead that waits hours is usually gone. Three habits help:

  • Reply within minutes during working hours. Set a short automatic greeting in WhatsApp Business for the rest of the time.
  • Save common answers (price, location, timings) as quick replies.
  • Note each lead in a sheet with the date and source, and follow up after two days if there is no answer.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Hiding the button below the fold on mobile.
  • Using a number that is not on WhatsApp Business, so you cannot label or automate chats.
  • Asking visitors to fill a long form and then also message you. Pick one main path.
  • Never checking which button or page produced the lead.

If you are choosing between a form and WhatsApp, run both for a month and compare. The source tag makes that comparison easy.
