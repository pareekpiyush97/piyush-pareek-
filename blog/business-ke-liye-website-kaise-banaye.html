Business ke liye website kaise banaye: 7 aasan steps

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

Website banana mushkil nahi hai, bas sahi order me kaam karna padta hai. Neeche 7 steps hain jo Bikaner aur poore India ke chhote business ke liye kaam karte hain, bina tech jargon ke.

In this article
  1. Step 1: goal tay karo
  2. Step 2: domain naam chuno
  3. Step 3: pages decide karo
  4. Step 4: mobile-first design
  5. Step 5: WhatsApp button lagao
  6. Step 6: Google Business Profile banao
  7. Step 7: launch karo aur track karo

Step 1: goal tay karo

Pehle socho ki website se kya chahiye: enquiry, booking, ya online sale? Goal ke hisaab se pages aur buttons decide hote hain.

Step 2: domain naam chuno

Chhota, yaad rehne wala naam lo, jaise aapke business ka naam. .com ya .in best rehta hai. Domain aapke naam par register ho, kisi aur ke naam par nahi.

Step 3: pages decide karo

Zyadatar business ke liye Home, Services, Gallery/Work, About aur Contact kaafi hain. Har page me apne shehar ka naam likho, jaise "Dentist in Bikaner".

Step 4: mobile-first design

Zyadatar log phone se dekhte hain. Text bada rakho, buttons aasani se tap hon, aur site tez khule. Heavy images compress karo.

Step 5: WhatsApp button lagao

Har page par WhatsApp button rakho, pehle se likha message ke saath. Poora tareeka yahan hai: website se WhatsApp enquiries kaise laye.

Step 6: Google Business Profile banao

Ye free hai aur Google Maps par dikhne ke liye zaroori hai. Naam, address aur phone number website jaisa hi rakho. Step-by-step: Google Business Profile setup guide.

Step 7: launch karo aur track karo

Launch ke baad Google Analytics aur UTM tags se dekho ki enquiry kahan se aa rahi hai. Phir har mahine content aur photos update karte raho.

Poora kharcha jaanna ho to website banwane ka kharcha padho.
