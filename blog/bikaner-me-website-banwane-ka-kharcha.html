Bikaner me website banwane ka kharcha: poori jaankari

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

Agar aap Bikaner me dukaan, clinic, hotel ya service business chalate ho aur sochte ho "website banwane me kitna kharcha aayega?", to jawab har jagah alag milta hai. Kahin ₹3,000, kahin ₹3 lakh. Is guide me simple bhasha me samjhaya hai ki price kis cheez se badalta hai, taaki aap quotes sahi tarah compare kar sako.

Seedha jawab: kitna kharcha aata hai

Ek standard business website ka one-time kharcha Zrimat me ₹6,999 se ₹14,999 ke beech hai. Online store (e-commerce) ya ERP/CRM jaise bade system ka price aapki zarurat dekh kar bataya jata hai, kyunki products, payment gateway aur modules ke hisaab se kaam badalta hai.

Plan ke hisaab se kya milta hai

PlanShuruaat priceKiske liyeKya milta hai
Starter₹6,999Portfolio, personal brandWork gallery, about aur contact, mobile-first design, WhatsApp button, Google Business setup
Growth₹14,999Enquiries chahne wale businessMultipage site, lead forms, WhatsApp enquiry routing, booking aur reminders, SEO
E-commerceQuote parOnline bechne waleCatalogue, cart, payment gateway, order tracking
ERP / CRMQuote parDaily operations wali teamSales pipeline, inventory, attendance, reports

Ye standard build ke starting prices hain. Pages aur features badhne par final price badalta hai, isliye likhit quote zaroor lo.

Kharcha kin cheezon se badhta hai

  • Pages ki sankhya: 5 page ki site 20 page se sasti hoti hai.
  • Custom design: aapke brand ke hisaab se bana design template se mehnga par aksar tez hota hai.
  • Features: booking, forms, WhatsApp routing aur payment gateway alag kaam hain.
  • Content: photos aur likhne ka kaam alag se plan karo. Apni dukaan ki asli photos stock photos se behtar hoti hain.

Wo kharche jo log bhool jaate hain

  1. Domain aur source code ka malik kaun hoga?
  2. Domain ka salana renewal kitna hai, aur hosting ho to uska?
  3. Launch ke baad badlav karwana ho to kya lagega?
  4. Quote fixed hai ya baad me badh sakta hai?

Zrimat me source code aapke apne GitHub par rehta hai, isliye aap kisi ke bandhan me nahi ho.

Sasti website vs achhi website

Sasti website galat nahi hoti, bas teen cheezein check karo: phone par sahi chalti hai? Tez khulti hai? Aapse contact karna aasan hai? Detail me dekhne ke liye hamari English cost guide aur 12-point checklist padho.

Quote kaise lein

Pages ki list banao, decide karo ki customer enquiry karega, booking karega ya khareedega, logo aur photos ready rakho, aur price aur delivery date likhit me mango. Zrimat free call par final price confirm karta hai. Shuru karne se pehle business ke liye website kaise banaye bhi padh lo.
