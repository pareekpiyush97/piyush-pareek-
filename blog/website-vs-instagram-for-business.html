Do you need a website if you already have Instagram?

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

Many small businesses in India run entirely on Instagram and WhatsApp, and it works for them. So is a website really needed? The honest answer is: it depends on how customers find you. Here is a simple way to decide.

In this article
  1. What Instagram does well
  2. What Instagram cannot do
  3. When you should get a website
  4. The best setup: both working together
  5. Cost and effort

What Instagram does well

Instagram is excellent for showing products and work, building a following and getting quick replies. If most of your customers already know you, or find you through reels and recommendations, it may be enough for now.

What Instagram cannot do

  • Be found on Google. People searching "dentist in Bikaner" or "wedding hotel near me" mostly see websites and Google Maps, not Instagram profiles.
  • Give you ownership. Reach, features and even the account can change. A website and domain belong to you.
  • Hold detail. Prices, services, location, timings and FAQs are easier to find on a page than in old posts.
  • Show trust to new customers. A site with your address, photos and reviews makes a stranger more comfortable.

When you should get a website

Build one if you answer yes to any of these: customers ask the same questions daily, you want enquiries from Google, you sell high-value services (hotel, clinic, property), or you run ads. For ads, a landing page almost always converts better than a profile.

The best setup: both working together

Keep Instagram for reach. Use the website for search, details and trust. Put the website in your bio, and add a WhatsApp button on the site so enquiries land in one chat. Tag your bio link with UTM so you can see how many leads Instagram sends, as explained in our WhatsApp enquiries guide.
