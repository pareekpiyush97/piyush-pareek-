Clinic website checklist: how to get more patient enquiries

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

Patients search for a clinic when they have a need, and they decide quickly. A clear website that answers the basic questions makes it easy for them to call or message. Here is what to include, and what to avoid.

In this article
  1. The essentials every clinic page needs
  2. Build trust carefully
  3. Make it findable
  4. Make it easy to reach you
  5. An example

The essentials every clinic page needs

  • Doctor profile: name, qualifications and registration details, with a real photo.
  • Services: one clear section per treatment or specialty, written in plain language.
  • Timings and location: a map, landmarks, parking and contact numbers.
  • Appointment request: a WhatsApp button and a short form for name, phone and preferred time.

Build trust carefully

Healthcare content needs care. Do not publish medical claims you cannot support, and follow the advertising rules that apply to your profession. Real photos of the clinic, your credentials and genuine patient feedback (with consent) build trust far better than big promises.

Make it findable

Put your city and specialty in the title and heading, for example "Homeopathy clinic in Bikaner". Set up your Google Business Profile with the same details, and keep the phone number identical everywhere.

Make it easy to reach you

Place the WhatsApp and call buttons where thumbs reach on mobile. Reply fast during clinic hours and use a saved greeting after hours. Our WhatsApp enquiries guide shows how to set this up and track where patients come from.
