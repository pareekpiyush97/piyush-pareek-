Google Business Profile kaise banaye: Google Maps par business kaise dale

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

Jab koi "meri nazdeek dukaan" ya "dentist in Bikaner" search karta hai, to Google Maps ki listing sabse pehle dikhti hai. Ye listing free hai. Aise banaye.

In this article
  1. 1. Listing claim ya create karo
  2. 2. Sahi category chuno
  3. 3. Address, phone aur timing bharo
  4. 4. Verify karo
  5. 5. Photos aur description
  6. 6. Reviews maango

1. Listing claim ya create karo

business.google.com par jao, apne business ka naam search karo. Mil jaye to claim karo, nahi to naya add karo. Naam wahi likho jo asli me hai.

2. Sahi category chuno

Sabse specific category lo, jaise "Homeopathic clinic". Zarurat ho to 2–3 secondary categories jodo.

3. Address, phone aur timing bharo

Website jaisa hi naam, address aur phone daalo. Map par exact pin lagao aur tyohar ki timing update rakho.

4. Verify karo

Google video, phone ya postcard se verify karwata hai. Verify hue bina profile poori tarah manage nahi hoti.

5. Photos aur description

Dukaan ke bahar, andar, team aur kaam ki asli photos daalo. Description saaf bhasha me likho, keywords thoonso mat.

6. Reviews maango

Khush customer ko WhatsApp par review link bhejo aur har review ka jawab do. Nakli reviews kabhi mat banwao.
