Hotel website design: 9 things that bring direct bookings

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

Guests find hotels through Google, Maps and booking portals, and then check the hotel's own website. If that site is slow or unclear, they go back to the portal and the commission is yours to pay. These nine things help a hotel website win the direct enquiry.

In this article
  1. What guests look for first
  2. What makes them contact you
  3. What helps you rank
  4. An example

What guests look for first

  1. Fast loading on mobile. Most travellers browse on phones, often on slow networks.
  2. Real photos of rooms and property. Compress them so pages stay fast.
  3. Clear room types and starting rates. Guests leave when they cannot find a price.
  4. Location and how to reach you. Embed a map and add landmarks and distances.

What makes them contact you

  1. A WhatsApp enquiry button on every page. Pre-fill the message with the room or date, as shown in our WhatsApp guide.
  2. A simple enquiry or booking form. Ask only for dates, guests and phone.
  3. Reviews and trust signals. Link to your Google reviews and show licences or awards you really hold.

What helps you rank

  1. Your city in titles and headings. For example "Heritage hotel in Bikaner". See the basics in the website checklist.
  2. A verified Google Business Profile with the same details. Follow our setup guide.

An example
