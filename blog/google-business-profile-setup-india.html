Google Business Profile setup: a step-by-step guide for India

By Piyush Pareek · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the free listing that shows your shop, clinic or hotel on Google Maps and in local search. For most local businesses it is the fastest way to get found. This guide walks through setting it up properly.

Step 1: create or claim your listing

Go to business.google.com, search for your business name and claim it if it already exists. If not, add it as a new business. Use your real, publicly known name, without extra keywords.

Step 2: choose the right category

Pick the most specific primary category, for example "Homeopathic clinic" instead of just "Clinic". Add a few secondary categories that truly apply. Category is one of the main signals Google uses to decide what you appear for.

Step 3: add accurate address, phone and hours

Use the same name, address and phone number as on your website. Add a pin on the exact location and mark special hours for festivals. Wrong hours lose customers and reviews.

Step 4: verify the profile

Google asks you to verify, usually by video, phone or a postcard, depending on the business. Complete it, since an unverified profile cannot be fully managed.

Step 5: add photos and a short description

Upload real photos of the front of your shop, inside, your team and your work. Write a plain description of what you do and where. Avoid stuffing keywords.

Step 6: ask for reviews, and reply

Share your review link with happy customers on WhatsApp after a sale or visit. Reply to every review, good or bad, politely. Never buy or invent reviews. It breaks Google policy and customers can tell.

Step 7: link it with your website

Add your website link and keep your profile information identical to the site. See our local business website checklist for the other basics, or read why a website still matters if you have Instagram.

You can also read Google's own guidance in the Business Profile Help Center.
